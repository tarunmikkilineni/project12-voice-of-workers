# Project 12 — Voice of Workers

## Research overview

**Topic:** Worker voice, algorithmic management, and procedural protections on digital labour platforms.

**Target journal:** Journal of Business Research.

This project investigates how digital labour platforms document workers' opportunities to contest decisions, receive explanations, communicate with human representatives, and participate in platform governance.

## Research design

The proposed study uses comparative documentary analysis of 15 digital labour platforms. It examines five research questions covering:

1. Configurations of procedural safeguards.
2. Platform discretion and worker protections.
3. Comparison with published Fairwork assessments.
4. Access barriers and account lockout.
5. Individual and collective worker voice.

## Data and preliminary findings

The preliminary analysis uses *Fairwork Cloudwork Ratings 2023*.

- Platforms included: 15.
- Mean published Fairwork score: 1.8 out of 10.
- Median published Fairwork score: 1 out of 10.
- Platforms awarded Fairwork threshold 4.1 for due process: 6 of 15 (40%).
- Candidate keyword matches identified in the report: 55.

These are secondary-data findings from a published report, not independently verified platform-policy coding results.
